## About

I am a mechanical engineering researcher working at the intersection of autonomous driving, deep learning, multimodal perception, and intelligent vehicle control. My doctoral research explores end-to-end prediction of steering angle and vehicle speed from camera and LiDAR observations, using CNN-based feature extraction, temporal modeling, and attention.

My background combines academic research and teaching with vehicle dynamics and practical automotive engineering experience. I am interested in postdoctoral research opportunities and academic collaborations in Canada.

## Featured Research

### [End-to-End Learning for Autonomous Driving](https://github.com/morteza-da/End-to-End-Learning-for-Autonomous-Driving)

My doctoral research investigates a multimodal architecture combining **Xception**, **ResNeXt-50**, **LSTM**, and **attention** to predict vehicle steering and speed. Monte Carlo dropout is also investigated for uncertainty estimation.

**Repository status:** Research documentation is available. The original local source files were lost, and code reconstruction is in progress. Reconstructed implementations and independently verified results will be clearly labeled.
